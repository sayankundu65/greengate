FAQs

Frequently asked questions

Key insights on buying, investing, and leasing property in Dubai.

Can foreign nationals buy freehold property in Dubai?

Yes. Non-residents and international investors enjoy 100% freehold ownership rights across designated master communities including Palm Jumeirah, Downtown Dubai, Dubai Marina, Dubai Hills Estate, and MBR City.

How do I qualify for the 10-Year UAE Golden Visa through property?

Purchasing ready or off-plan property valued at AED 2,000,000 or above qualifies the owner and their immediate family for a renewable 10-year UAE Golden Visa. Green Gate handles the full nomination and DLD application process.

What are the typical payment plans for off-plan projects in Dubai?

Leading developers offer interest-free milestone schedules such as 60/40, 70/30, or 80/20—requiring only 10% to 20% down payment upon booking and the final balance upon handover, protected in DLD-regulated escrow accounts.

Are rental yields and capital gains taxed in the UAE?

No. Residential real estate in Dubai benefits from 0% annual property tax, 0% rental income tax, and 0% capital gains tax, allowing investors to retain 100% of their net returns.
