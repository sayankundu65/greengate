Green Gate Real Estate LLC Privacy Policy

Contact Us

Effective Date: August 2023 (Updated November 2026)

Green Gate Real Estate LLC ("Greengate", "we", "us", or "our") respects the privacy of our users ("you" or "your"). This Privacy Policy describes the types of information we collect from and about you when you visit our website or interact with our real estate brokerage services in Dubai, United Arab Emirates, and how we use, disclose, and protect that information.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide through our property inquiry, brochure request, consultation, and career forms, including: Full Name, Phone / WhatsApp Number, Email Address, City / Country of Residence, Job Title, and Property Preferences.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To fulfill your requests and connect you with luxury villas, apartments, townhouses, and off-plan investments in Dubai that match your criteria.
  • To share requested floor plans, developer payment schedules, and Dubai Land Department (DLD) market updates.
  • To improve our website performance, user experience, and advisory services.
  • To comply with UAE Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021 on the Protection of Personal Data (PDPL), RERA, and DLD regulatory requirements.

3. Data Retention & Security

We retain your information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined above or as required by UAE law. We implement strict administrative and technical safeguards and never sell or rent client contact details to unauthorized third parties.

4. Your Choices & Contact Information
